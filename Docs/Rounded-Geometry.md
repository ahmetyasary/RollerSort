# Rounded board and conveyor geometry

The four-column pocket's internal riser omitted the 14-unit pocket allowance used by its outer edge. The riser therefore cut through the second column's source boxes. Its x coordinate now includes that allowance, leaving seven units between the source silhouette and the wall centre. Pocket corners use six-unit quadratic rounds. The existing smooth upper bowl curve is preserved.

The conveyor asset now uses its actual runtime dimensions (326 × 69). Its semicircular ends share the runtime stadium's straight half-length of 120 and radius of 23. The moving decorative sockets are circular 19 × 19 images, replacing stretched 19 × 21 rounded rectangles. Marble motion and contact constraints are unchanged.

Editable SVGs and 3× runtime PNGs are generated by Tools/refine-board-belt.mjs, also invoked at the end of the base art generator. A signed simulator Release build passed and was installed on iPhone 18 Pro Max. Native screenshot: rounded-geometry-simulator.png.
